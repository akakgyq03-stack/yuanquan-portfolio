# Portfolio surface brief

## Scope and mode

Experience mode. Home plus nine long-form project routes.

## Audience and job

Recruiters and interviewers need to understand the candidate's range, inspect evidence quickly, share a project URL, and find contact information.

## Constraints

Figma is the approved visual authority. Preserve factual copy and visible project assets. Publish email only. Hidden Figma work areas are excluded.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The work is the interface. Preserve each project's own visual world while a restrained editorial navigation system links them together; refuse a generic portfolio card grid as the main experience.

**OWN-WORLD:** Near-black home and AIGC surfaces, project-specific palettes for case studies, white typography, hairline rules, large bilingual titles, full-bleed authored imagery, and precise editorial grids.

**STORY:** Visitors begin with capability and evidence indexes, enter a project, move along its chapter axis, and leave through adjacent projects or the work index.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** The home opens with contact, identity, positioning, and three evidence categories at the Figma scale. Long-form case studies open with their authored hero and a directory axis directly below the global navigation; AIGC and art showcase routes remain uninterrupted.

**FORM:** Approved Figma composition; no concept replacement. Where present, the directory axis is sticky, hash-addressable, and responsive. Figma file key `gYBF4Qe1l8IdWkbvpNkmCX` is the measured reference.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
